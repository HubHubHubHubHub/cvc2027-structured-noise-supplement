# S1 — Aggregate counts from the closing sweeps

Alongside the per-entry re-audit, the six closing passes each swept
their family for works absent from the corpus and eligible under a
*literal* reading of the consideration criteria. In aggregate, the
sweeps identified approximately 69 unique such works, all first
available before 27 August 2026; the strict 27–31 August window was
empty in all six families.

By category (approximate one-third each):

1. **Sampler-convergence and discrete/masked error analyses** —
   eligible under criterion (ii) read literally; this literature is
   represented in the corpus by selected landmarks only, as stated in
   the paper.
2. **Sampler, initialisation, and coupling designs** — eligible only
   under a broad reading of the preconditioning/latent loci.
3. **Structural forward modifications** — dominated by two families
   deliberately kept outside the curated scope: temporally correlated
   video noise (a family of its own), and heavy-tailed /
   jump / subordinated forward processes, represented through the
   non-local-generator landmarks already coded.

No title-level listing is provided: the corpus is curated rather than
exhaustive, and its absence claims are corpus-relative and dated
(paper, Section 3, Review scope and methodology). These aggregate counts are recorded for auditability of
the curation decision itself.
