# Supplement S1 — Audit record of the survey corpus

Companion to the anonymised submission *Structured Noise in Diffusion
Models: An Audited Survey with a Finite-Dimensional Protocol for
Calibrated Error Propagation* (double-blind). This supplement is a verification
instrument: the paper is self-contained without it.

Contents:

- `S1_search_record.md` — the eight declared search families, their
  exact re-executed queries, and dated identification counts.
- `S1_row_level_audit_log.md` — the closing audit of all 47 corpus
  entries: per-entry verdict, correction (if any), and primary source.
- `S1_aggregate_counts.md` — aggregate counts of works identified by
  the closing sweeps as eligible under a literal reading of the
  criteria but outside the curated scope, by category (no title-level
  listing; the corpus is curated rather than exhaustive, as stated in
  the paper).

Timeline: corpus initially assembled on 27 August 2026; formally
closed on 31 August 2026 after a dated rerun of all search families
and an independent primary-text re-audit of all 47 entries. The
intervening window (28–31 August) yielded no eligible addition and no
source revision.

Note on numbering: in the submitted paper the full audited corpus
appears as Table 1; the earlier representative table of the audited
draft was merged into it.
